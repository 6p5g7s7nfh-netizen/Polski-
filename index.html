🧠 Spaced Repetition (SRS)
Cards you find hard come back sooner. Easy cards return after longer gaps. This mirrors how memory consolidates.

🔁 Active Recall
See Polish → recall English (or vice versa). Don't peek — the struggle to remember is the learning.

🗂 Category Chunking
Words grouped by theme (numbers, greetings, time) are easier to store and retrieve together.

🎯 10 New + 5 Review
Each session introduces new words and revisits previous ones. Review prevents forgetting.
